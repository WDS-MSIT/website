Internship Cell

   

Industry-Institution-Interaction Cell (IIIC)

The Industry-Institution-Interaction Cell (IIIC) at Maharaja Surajmal Institute of Technology (MSIT) serves as a dynamic bridge between academic excellence and industrial innovation. Established under AICTE guidelines.In today's rapidly evolving technological landscape, classroom learning alone isn't enough. IIIC ensures that MSIT students:

  • Gain hands-on experience with cutting-edge technologies
  • Develop industry-relevant skills through real-world projects
  • Build professional networks before graduation
  • Understand market expectations and industry standards

The Core Functions of IIIC include:

  • Comprehensive summer internship programs with leading companies.
  • In-house industrial training opportunities.
  • Management of AICTE Internship Portal. (https://internship.aicte-india.org)
  • Year-round internship opportunities for enhanced employability.
  • Development and execution of MoUs with premier companies.
  • Facilitation of long-term industry-institute collaborations.
  • Expert lectures from industry leaders.
  • Specialized seminars and skill development programs.
  • Faculty development programs aligned with industry trends.
  • Organized industrial visits for practical exposure.

Strategic Partnerships & MOUs

IIIC has MOUs with some of the leading government and private organizations which aim towards the skill development, research, guest lectures, summer training and industrial visits. Our MOU partners are Airports Authority of India, Tata Power Delhi Distribution Ltd., NIELIT (National Institute of Electronics and Information Technology), ONGC Ltd., Brain Mentors Private Ltd., AI-SHALA Private. Ltd., The IoT Academy and PanTech e-learning.
